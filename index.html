About

01 / FILE

I'm a 26-year-old engineer focused on AI vision and computer vision — diffusion models, sensor synchronization, and deep learning for image reconstruction. My training is in medical systems engineering at ESIGELEC (Rouen, ISYMED dominant), which gave me a strong applied base in embedded devices and AI, and my recent work has mostly been applied in clinical imaging — though the underlying skills (model design, multi-sensor systems, real-time capture) carry directly into computer vision work more broadly.

Recent projects: exploring whether diffusion models (DDIM) can improve partial-volume correction in ¹⁷⁷Lu SPECT/CT by integrating them into an existing R2U-Net pipeline, and building a synchronized multi-sensor capture system controlling DVSense and DAVIS346 event cameras for motion and scene-change analysis. Both were built at the Computer Vision Laboratory, University of Würzburg — the SPECT work in direct partnership with Universitätsklinikum Würzburg's medical imaging team.

Earlier academic projects — a first-prize implantable inertial motion tracker for patients with impaired motor function, a CNN trained to classify pneumonia from chest X-rays, a MobileNetV2-based facial detection pipeline, and a line-following robot pair built on an MSP430 — trace a consistent thread: taking a diagnostic, clinical, or sensing problem and building the model or device that solves it end to end, from embedded hardware up through the training pipeline.

I work primarily in Python and PyTorch, with CUDA, OpenCV, and Arduino/MATLAB for the embedded and signal-processing side, and I'm fluent across Chinese (native), English (TOEIC 860 / B2), and French (DELF B2).
